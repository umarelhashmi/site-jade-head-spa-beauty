## Product Principles

1. **Tactile Proof Over Adjective Inflation**: Let real photography of the Golden Ring Waterfall arches, Shiseido foam rituals, twin-brush facials, and exact menu pricing speak instead of generic spa clichés.
2. **Zero-Friction Menu Discovery**: Make all 30 treatments across 8 categories effortless to scan, compare, and book without burying prices or forcing endless scrolling.
3. **Intimate Architectural Calm**: Every screen—desktop and mobile—should feel like stepping into a quiet, bespoke beauty sanctuary rather than a SaaS marketing template.
