# Design System: Jade Head Spa & Beauty

## Creative Direction: "Porcelain Apothecary & Ryokan Menu Book"

Jade Head Spa & Beauty’s interface is grounded in the physical materials of its Kissimmee sanctuary: warm porcelain basins, brushed brass 360-degree water arches, camellia-blush suite walls, sage-celadon embroidered towels, and Japanese Shiseido botanical apothecary vessels.

Instead of a rigid grid of bordered boxes or generic SaaS cards, the page flows like a quiet visit to a Japanese bathhouse and apothecary:
1. **Borderless Apothecary Still-Lifes**: Studio cutout objects (`assets/images/ai/cutout-*.png`) and the lotus emblem (`logo-emblem.png`) sit on pure porcelain surfaces with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so their backgrounds dissolve completely, allowing the brass water arch, Shiseido bottles, jade comb, and jasmine tea tray to rest naturally on the canvas without boxy frames.
2. **The Open Linen Menu Book**: All 30 official treatments across 8 categories are presented in an open, two-column spa menu book with classic dotted price leaders, instant category bookmarks, a solo/couples switch, and live search.
3. **Interactive 3-Tap Ritual Matcher**: First-time guests can filter by guest count (One Guest vs. Two Guests), treatment focus (Waterfall Head Spa, Shiseido Special, Korean & Gentleman's Facial, Body & Foot Massage), and time available (30, 60, or 90+ minutes) to immediately see matching rituals.

## Color Palette

- **Porcelain Mist (`#FAF7F2`)**: Primary warm rice-paper canvas.
- **Pure Vessel (`#FFFFFF`)**: Ceramic surface for apothecary object showcases and the open menu book.
- **Peony Blush Wash (`#F4E8E4`)**: Soft rosewater surface inspired by the Kissimmee treatment suites and fresh pink roses.
- **Celadon Linen (`#E5EDE8`)**: Mineral sage surface inspired by the embroidered Jade Spa towels.
- **Deep Nephrite (`#132A22`)**: Primary typographic ink on light surfaces and nocturnal sanctuary background for the Couples Retreat chapter.
- **Warm Brass Halo (`#B58646`)**: Brushed brass accent echoing the 360-degree Golden Ring Waterfall arch.

## Typography

- **Editorial & Display Serif**: `Newsreader` (optical size 6–72pt, weights 300, 400, 500, and italic) — warm, literary, and tranquil.
- **Interface & Reading Sans**: `Instrument Sans` (weights 400, 500, 600) — clean, open, and effortless to scan across durations, prices, and controls.
- **Typographic Discipline**: Sentence-case headings and navigation, no tracked-out uppercase eyebrow clichés above headings, no single-word color/italic gimmicks inside headlines, and `tabular-nums` on all prices and durations.
