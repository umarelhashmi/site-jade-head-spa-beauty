What happens during a Golden Ring Waterfall Head Spa.

Whether you book a 30-minute refresh ($80), our classic 60-minute session ($120), or the 90-minute Japanese Shiseido package ($199), your visit is designed around pure comfort and relaxation.

  1. Step 1

    Warm welcome & scalp relaxation

    Settle onto a heated treatment bed wrapped in a soft Jade towel while your specialist eases tension across your head, temples, and neck.

  2. Step 2

    Deep scalp cleansing & soothing head massage

    Warm, rich lather gently cleanses the scalp and hair while unhurried hand massage relieves everyday stress and tightness.

  3. Step 3

    Golden Ring Waterfall Therapy

    Our signature golden water arch streams warm, gentle water across your scalp and hair for deeply soothing hydrotherapy.

  4. Step 4

    Nourishing hair conditioning & warm steam

    Conditioning care—or authentic Japanese Shiseido hair treatment on our Special Menu—leaves your hair soft, clean, and refreshed.

  5. Step 5

    Warm towel wrap & neck and shoulder relief

    Your session finishes with a warm towel compress and relaxing neck and shoulder massage so you leave feeling rested and renewed.

Questions before your visit

What should I expect during a Golden Ring Waterfall Head Spa?

You lie comfortably on a heated treatment bed while warm water streams gently from our Golden Ring Waterfall arch over your scalp and hair. Your specialist performs a soothing scalp massage, deep cleansing, conditioning, and neck and shoulder relaxation.

How is the Japanese Shiseido Package different from the standard Head Spa?

Our Special Menu uses authentic Japanese Shiseido professional scalp and hair care formulations paired with extended body massage—available in 60-minute ($159 solo / $318 for two) or 90-minute ($199 solo / $398 for two) sessions.

How do we reserve a side-by-side Couples Head Spa or Couple Massage?

You can book any of our 7 Couple Head Spa packages ($240–$360), Shiseido For Two ($318–$398), or Couple Massage sessions ($160–$260) directly online through ByChronos, or call our reception desk at (407) 989-3326 and we will reserve a private two-bed suite for you.

Do I need to do anything to prepare before arriving?

Come just as you are. Avoid heavy hair styling sprays if you are visiting for a Head Spa, and arrive 5 to 10 minutes before your appointment to settle in at our reception lounge.
