What happens inside a Golden Ring Waterfall session.

Whether you choose an express 30-minute refresh ($80), our classic 60-minute ritual ($120), or the 90-minute Shiseido package ($199), your time in the suite unfolds as an unhurried five-part progression.

  1. Step 1

    Warm botanical oil & scalp preparation

    You recline on a heated bed wrapped in an embroidered Jade towel while your specialist eases tension at the temples and prepares the scalp with aromatic botanical oils.

  2. Step 2

    Cloud-foam cleansing & meridian acupressure

    Rich, warm lather clears buildup around the hair follicles while slow thumb and finger pressure works along the cranial, neck, and sub-occipital meridian lines.

  3. Step 3

    360° Golden Ring Waterfall immersion

    The brass halo arch pivots gently over the porcelain basin, bathing your crown and hair in dozens of silky, temperature-controlled streams of warm water.

  4. Step 4

    Conditioning mist & nourishing hair bath

    Warm ozone mist opens the hair cuticle so Japanese Shiseido or botanical conditioning treatments absorb deeply, leaving hair soft, light, and luminous.

  5. Step 5

    Steamed towel wrap, shoulder release & tea

    Your ritual concludes with a warm towel compress, neck and shoulder massage, and a quiet moment to transition before stepping back into your day.

Questions before your visit

What should I expect during a Golden Ring Waterfall Head Spa?

You lie comfortably on a heated treatment bed while warm water arcs in a gentle 360-degree halo over your scalp and hair. Your specialist performs a botanical scalp cleanse, foaming cranial meridian acupressure, conditioning steam, and neck and shoulder relief.

How is the Japanese Shiseido Package different from the standard Head Spa?

Our Special Menu uses authentic Japanese Shiseido professional scalp and hair care formulations paired with extended body massage—available in 60-minute ($159 solo / $318 for two) or 90-minute ($199 solo / $398 for two) sessions.

How do we reserve a side-by-side Couples Head Spa or Couple Massage?

You can book any of our 7 Couple Head Spa packages ($240–$360), Shiseido For Two ($318–$398), or Couple Massage sessions ($160–$260) directly online through ByChronos, or call our reception desk at (407) 989-3326 and we will reserve a private two-bed suite for you.

Do I need to do anything to prepare before arriving?

Come just as you are. Avoid heavy hair styling sprays if you are visiting for a Head Spa, and arrive 5 to 10 minutes before your appointment to settle in at our reception lounge.
